# NOTICE — attribution and provenance

## Ownership of this package
The `mlip_evidence` tooling, its tests, the synthetic examples, and the documentation in
this package were authored for Kazi Ehsanul Karim's research website. The **scientific
decisions, validation design, reference protocol, and scope judgments are the author's**.
The analysis code was **drafted with AI assistance** and checked against raw outputs; a
passing test count demonstrates software behaviour, not scientific validity or physical
accuracy.

## What is included vs. referenced
- **Included:** new, self-contained tooling + **synthetic** fixtures + methods/scope docs.
- **Referenced only (NOT included):** the DFT/MLIP campaign's inputs, models, trajectories,
  logs, and numerical results; these are private research artifacts.

## Upstream software (used in the underlying research; not distributed here)
These tools were used to produce the campaign that this package *describes*; each is the
property of its authors under its own license, and none is redistributed in this package:
- **CP2K** — Quickstep DFT (GPL). https://www.cp2k.org
- **DeePMD-kit** — MLIP training/inference (LGPL-3.0). https://github.com/deepmodeling/deepmd-kit
- **DP-GEN** — concurrent-learning workflow (LGPL-3.0). https://github.com/deepmodeling/dpgen
- **LAMMPS** — molecular dynamics (GPL-2.0). https://www.lammps.org
- **NumPy**, **Matplotlib**, **SciPy** — scientific Python (BSD/PSF-style licenses).

## Constants
Physical constants are NIST CODATA 2022 (checked 2026-09-06). The force-conversion factor
is derived from the two primary constants rather than hard-coded.

## Data statement
No experimental or third-party dataset is redistributed. All numeric fixtures are
synthetic and generated by `examples/synthetic/make_fixture.py`. Published prior work by
the author (e.g. PRE 2021, PCCP 2024) belongs to the respective journals and is cited, not
included.

## Excluded by policy
Private research results, model checkpoints, raw trajectories/logs, the readiness plan,
and any PINN code/results are intentionally excluded from this package.
