## Molecular calibration pilot (functionality only, not a measurement)

Functionality only; no number here enters the lab. LAMMPS (22 Jul 2025 - Update 4) on one core. The builder reproduces the same data file (SHA-256 59315c32…) from the default seed: 4661 atoms (842 fixed carbons, 1273 SPC/E waters), cell 2.95 nm × 2.98 nm × 7.10 nm. The equilibration stage (minimisation, 2 ps limited-displacement start, NVT) and a driven stage restarted from it (fz = 0.010 kcal mol⁻¹ Å⁻¹, 3 ps) ran to completion, and crossings_from_dump.py wrote the crossing CSV read by analyze_pore_flux.py (62 rows). Speed 2.73–3.06 ns/day on one core of a shared machine, so the campaign (480 ns of production) needs about 157–176 single-core days; its 24 runs are independent. Filling was not reached: in 36 ps from the empty tube, the number of oxygens inside the tube averaged 0.42 between 10 and 36 ps (largest 1 ps mean 1.0; none in 31 % of the 1 ps windows), whereas a hydrogen-bonded single file (O–O ≈ 0.28 nm) would hold about nine.

Since release 2026.8 the campaign has eight zero-drive replicas (560 ns of production, 28 runs, about 183–205 single-core days at the pilot speed) because the equilibrium gate of `analyze_pore_flux.py` needs G_eq to 10 % (tests A4, A5).
