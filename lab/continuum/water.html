The membrane-flow experiment has been retired from the public site

The water-flow-through-a-membrane experiment that was at this address was retired on 16 November 2026 (website release 2026.12.2). The Continuum Lab now presents one focused case: water flow in a carbon nanotube, described by a continuum model with molecular boundary inputs and compared with molecular dynamics from my published research.

Open the Continuum Lab

Its source code, provenance and complete validation record are kept in a development archive outside the website: 50 of 53 checks passed, and the three comparisons with OpenFOAM (F2–F4) failed and are recorded as failures. Earlier versions also remain in the public history of the site’s repository.
